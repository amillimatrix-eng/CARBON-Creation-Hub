## Correction

A control cannot be scored FAIL when the run did not establish a bounded exhaustive measurement of the condition.

The following prior evidence statements are absence-of-proof statements:
- no current accepted scope/price evidenced;
- no current contracted work evidenced;
- no current invoice/receivable evidenced;
- no current paid/settled outcome evidenced;
- no complete current discovery→settlement case evidenced.

Those statements prove only:
**NOT PROVEN IN THE OBSERVED EVIDENCE SET.**

They do not prove:
**THE OPPOSITE STATE EXISTS.**

This follows existing AMX rules:
- UNKNOWN/HOLD over unsupported inference;
- missing benchmark/evidence is not negative evidence;
- evidence supremacy;
- lifecycle truth;
- assumptions carry zero weight;
- provider/canonical state may be incomplete or temporarily desynchronized.

## Correct status model

- PASS — defined condition directly evidenced.
- FAIL — defined condition was actually measured within a bounded observable population and contrary/noncompliant evidence was observed.
- PARTIAL — measured subconditions include both satisfied and unsatisfied/unfinished elements.
- UNMEASURED_NOT_PROVEN — current evidence cannot establish the condition either way.
- UNRANKED — no defensible comparative benchmark/population exists.

UNMEASURED_NOT_PROVEN does not enter the numeric failure denominator.
