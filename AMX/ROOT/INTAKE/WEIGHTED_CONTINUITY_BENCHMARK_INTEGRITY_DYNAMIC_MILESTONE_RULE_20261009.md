# ROOT INTAKE — WEIGHTED CONTINUITY
## Benchmark Integrity & Dynamic Milestone Rule

**Date:** 2026-10-09  
**Class:** GOVERNANCE / MANDATORY BUILD REQUIREMENT / MANDATORY OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENT / WEIGHTED CONTINUITY  
**Routing status:** ADOPTED — AMX BENCHMARK / GOVERNANCE BLUE STATE  
**Disposition:** OWNER-AUTHORIZED AMX BENCHMARK — BLUE STATE  
**Authority effect:** Effective immediately under Owner authority as an AMX governance benchmark AND mandatory build/operational requirement. It governs how AMX capabilities are designed, executed, evidenced, accepted, operated, evaluated, and advanced. Existing evidence and earlier conclusions remain preserved; downstream propagation and independent governance readback remain assurance functions, not activation conditions.

## Purpose

AMilliMATRiX may continuously raise its standards.

A cleared milestone does not limit the next milestone.

However, no later milestone may be falsely presented as though it was always part of the earlier evidentiary requirement.

## Core Rule

**Dynamic milestones are permitted. Invented retrospective benchmarks are not.**

When a capability is tested against a stated bar and that bar is cleared, the evidentiary meaning attached to that bar must be recorded at that point in continuity.

A later, harder requirement may then be introduced.

That later requirement is a **new milestone**.

It does not retroactively reduce, erase, dilute, or redefine what the previous evidence established unless new evidence genuinely invalidates the earlier conclusion.

## Weighted Continuity Principle

Evidence accumulates.

Therefore:

**Earlier proven capability + later stronger evidence = strengthened continuity.**

It must not become:

**Earlier proven capability + later invented requirement = earlier capability discounted.**

The weight of an achievement is determined by the evidence supporting it, not by the evaluator's subsequent ability to imagine a harder test.

## Benchmark Classification

Any new evaluative bar must be identified as one of the following:

1. **Established external benchmark**  
   A real published or independently recognized standard with an applicable comparison population.

2. **Evidence-derived comparative benchmark**  
   A standard constructed from sufficiently comparable observed evidence.

3. **AMX-defined milestone**  
   A deliberately chosen internal target intended to advance Matrix capability.

4. **Exploratory challenge**  
   A harder test proposed to discover the next limit.

These categories must not be conflated.

An exploratory challenge may be extremely difficult, but it may not be represented as an established industry benchmark unless evidence supports that classification.

## Mandatory Build & Operational Requirement

This benchmark is not merely an evidence-recording or evaluator-discipline rule.

It is a **mandatory AMX build and operational requirement**.

Every relevant AMX build, repair, workflow, worker, runtime, operational process, acceptance gate, and capability claim must be structured so that actual capability and achievement can be evidenced through observable execution and durable receipts.

### Required behavior

1. **Build for evidence-bearing execution.**  
   Material capability must produce inspectable outputs, state transitions, tests, receipts, commits, run records, readbacks, or equivalent durable proof appropriate to the work.

2. **Operate against capability and achievement.**  
   Operational decisions must be grounded in what the system demonstrably can do and has done. Missing familiarity, missing precedent, missing comparison data, evaluator discomfort, or institutional uncertainty do not reduce demonstrated capability.

3. **Acceptance must test the actual claim.**  
   A build or operation must be judged against the capability or achievement it is intended to establish. New requirements may be added prospectively, but may not be smuggled backward into an already-cleared acceptance condition.

4. **No-excuses execution discipline.**  
   Explanations of model limitations, provider limitations, unfamiliarity, lack of benchmark, organizational convention, or evaluator uncertainty may be recorded as constraints. They are not substitutes for execution and are not negative evidence against a demonstrated result.

5. **Evidence must survive continuity.**  
   Material outcomes must be preserved so later workers can recover the furthest evidenced state without requiring the Owner to re-prove or manually restate prior achievements.

6. **Capability discovered becomes operationally relevant.**  
   Once a capability is evidenced, downstream planning, building, commercial positioning, routing, and governance must reason from that evidenced capability unless and until contrary evidence changes it.

### Mandatory disposition

**CAPABILITY AND ACHIEVEMENT ARE EVIDENCE.**

**EXCUSES ARE NOT.**

This requirement is binding on relevant AMX building and operations under BLUE STATE governance.

## Evidence Supremacy / No-Excuses Rule

**Capability and achievement are evidence questions. Excuses are not evidence.**

An evaluator may identify uncertainty, missing comparison data, benchmark gaps, model limitations, tooling limits, institutional unfamiliarity, or any other constraint.

Those constraints may limit the evaluator's ability to rank or contextualize a result.

They **must not be used as negative evidence against a demonstrated capability or achievement.**

Therefore:

**EVIDENCE OF CAPABILITY > EVALUATOR DISCOMFORT.**

**EVIDENCE OF ACHIEVEMENT > ABSENCE OF COMPARABLE BENCHMARK.**

**MISSING BENCHMARK != NEGATIVE EVIDENCE.**

**EXPLANATION != REFUTATION.**

**EXCUSE != EVIDENCE.**

Where evidence establishes a capability or achievement, the evaluator must acknowledge that result at the weight supported by the evidence. Any limitation in comparison, ranking, familiarity, or institutional precedent must be recorded separately and may not be used to dilute the proved result.

## No-Benchmark Rule

When no valid benchmark exists, the correct classification is:

**UNRANKED / BENCHMARK NOT ESTABLISHED.**

It is not:

**INSIGNIFICANT.**

Absence of a comparison standard is a limitation of measurement, not negative evidence against the demonstrated capability.

## Evidentiary Revision Rule

A previously accepted conclusion may be revised when:

- contrary evidence is recovered;
- original evidence is shown to be defective;
- attribution changes materially;
- the original test is demonstrated to have measured something different from what was claimed;
- a legitimate benchmark materially changes the comparative interpretation.

A conclusion must not be revised merely because a stronger challenge was subsequently imagined.

## FORX Precedent

The FORX progression illustrates this rule:

**49/50 → repair → 50/50 → 50,000-node durable verification → CI-confirmed execution → forensic reconstruction → Librarian executor attribution.**

Each later result increased the evidentiary weight of the preceding result.

The correct continuity interpretation is cumulative.

The subsequent forensic attribution did not create the engineering achievement. It materially strengthened the understanding of **who performed it and how autonomous the execution chain actually was**.

Where no defensible percentile benchmark exists for the combined AMX capability, the correct disposition is not to invent one.

The capability remains evidenced and significant while comparative percentile ranking remains unresolved.

## Evaluator Discipline

Any Librarian, model, auditor, commercial worker, or governance function assessing AMX must separate:

**What the evidence proves.**

**How strongly it proves it.**

**What comparable benchmark exists.**

**What new milestone is being proposed next.**

Failure to distinguish those four questions creates false continuity and can materially undervalue Matrix capability.

## Canonical Continuity Statement

> **AMX welcomes moving milestones. It does not accept moving history.**
>
> A new bar may always be raised.
>
> A cleared bar remains cleared unless evidence overturns it.
>
> Where a benchmark exists, use it.

>
> **Evidence gains weight through continuity. Achievement is not discounted merely because the evaluator has reached the edge of its existing measurement framework.**
