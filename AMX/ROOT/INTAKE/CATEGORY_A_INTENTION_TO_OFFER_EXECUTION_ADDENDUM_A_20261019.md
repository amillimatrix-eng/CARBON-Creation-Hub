## 1. Owner intent and governing commercial invariant

The Owner's direction is that capability cannot generate income while it remains unoffered. AMX must actively discover relevant buyers, derive buyer-appropriate offers from capabilities it can actually deliver, make permitted approaches at a sustainable volume, learn from qualified outcomes, improve conversion, and trace accepted work to verified settlement.

**No offer presented = no buyer can accept that offer.** A catalogue left unused is not an operating revenue strategy. Reports, candidate lists, polished positioning, page views, drafts and internal agreement do not substitute for buyer-facing offers.

This is not permission to spam, duplicate contacts, invent a service, misstate readiness, or pressure a buyer. It is a direction to make the existing commercial system productive: continuous offer generation and buyer approach, constrained by evidence, relevance, consent/contact rules, scope, delivery capacity, authority and economics.

**Core execution loop:**  
`CAPABILITY + BUYER SIGNAL → INTENTION HYPOTHESIS → BUYER / PROBLEM / AUTHORITY CHECK → OFFER CANDIDATES → EVIDENCE + FEASIBILITY + ECONOMICS → BUYER-SPECIFIC OFFER → AUTHORIZED APPROACH → RESPONSE / FOLLOW-UP → ACCEPTED SCOPE → VERIFIED PAYMENT / SETTLEMENT → DELIVERY / ACCEPTANCE → ATTRIBUTED LEARNING → NEXT OFFER`

Do not stop at search, qualification, a draft, a catalogue, an acknowledgement or a tool run. Continue to the furthest lawful, useful, evidence-backed next action.

## 2. Apply existing law; do not create another sales system

The method already exists in the intention algorithm, the Matrix-wide integration directive, the Carbon Intent-to-Close flight, the offer-quality / outcome-attribution repair, the commercial-routing control and the pricing register. The defect is **consumption, execution and outcome readback**, not lack of another document.

Apply these existing rules together:
- **Intent before literalism:** infer the practical outcome behind a request, but keep inference as a hypothesis until evidenced.
- **DON'T ASSUME. KNOW.** Resolve material unknowns using available authorized sources. Ask only when the unresolved answer would change a consequential decision.
- **Observed difference ≠ buyer pain ≠ purchase intent.** Confirm materiality; do not diagnose a buyer's motives or budget from aesthetics, size, public wording or job title.
- **Claim ≤ evidence; do not down-sell evidence.** State the strongest commercially material bounded capability that current proof supports; do not overstate readiness, and do not weaken good proof merely because it is unusual.
- **Existing single-writer route:** FORX/source discovery → iSCOPE (discover, verify, dedupe, qualify, durable opportunity evidence) → PRI (all outgoing propositions and post-handoff progression) → existing delivery owner (feasibility/delivery) → Banker (cost, margin, tax/invoice and verified settlement). Carbon Studios is a production capability/owner, not a parallel sales writer.
- **Offer identity and truth:** use the existing `proposal_ref` / Offer ID and immutable version. A customer-facing quote/pro-forma, sent receipt, buyer acceptance, invoice, payment and settlement are distinct states. No receipt = no closure.
- **Existing allocation rules remain separate:** 65% matrix-wide KPI weight for attributable contribution toward verified money-in / removal of a real blocker is not a time quota; the existing 60/20/20 sourcing allocation is a separate effort rule. Inbound/accepted offers progress from their furthest verified stage and are not delayed by a sourcing allocation.
- **Pricing remains governed:** use the existing market/currency/scope benchmark and current Blue State. South Africa's effective factor for new offers is 50% of a defensible comparable low-end benchmark from 2026-10-19; other markets retain the applicable 67% rule unless Owner-superseded. No invented comparator, FX-only localization, silent reprice or mass reissue.

## 3. Mandatory capability-to-offer pass

For each materially evidenced AMX capability, product, method, creative asset, production asset or service, the existing commercial inventory must be checked for:
1. **What exists:** actual deliverable, current state, owner, supporting artifact and strongest evidence.
2. **Who buys it:** distinct plausible buyer groups, their jobs-to-be-done, purchase authority, trigger, constraints and acceptable buying route.
3. **What problem/outcome it serves:** observed fact, hypothesis, counter-evidence, confidence, freshness and unknowns separated.
4. **What can be sold now:** the smallest useful finished output that is deliverable with current capability, without representing a future build as live.
5. **What adjacent offers are viable:** other buyer types, stages, packaging, licensing, white-label, maintenance, training, reporting, adaptation or recurring service only when the buyer need and AMX ability are supported.
6. **What is not sale-ready:** explicit reason, owner, dependency and next review trigger; do not bury opportunity in an indefinite “planned” label.
7. **What was offered:** target cohort, exact offer/version, evidence used, approach route, message/send receipt, next-action and due state.
8. **What happened:** delivery, response, objection, referral, rejection, acceptance, invoice, verified payment/settlement and delivery acceptance, each with source evidence.

A capability with credible evidence and a plausible buyer must not remain invisible merely because nobody explicitly asks for its name. Conversely, existence of a tool or concept alone does not authorize a production claim. The pass should produce either a bounded offer ready for iSCOPE/PRI qualification, or a precise hold reason. “No action” requires evidence that no permitted, useful next step exists.

## 4. Opportunity and offer construction

For every signal, examine the business/product as a system—not just a contact scrape. Use lawful/public or authorized evidence: product and service surfaces, pricing/monetization, update/release signals, customer/review questions, onboarding, listings, public claims vs actual visible product, booking/payment/contact paths, support routes, hiring/team signals, channel performance where trustworthy, and competitor/substitute options. Treat style or apparent process inconsistency as a clue to investigate, not proof of a defect or buyer intent.

Generate more than one plausible resolution when appropriate. Compare options on:
- relevance to the buyer's actual job and current signal;
- consequence / value of the outcome to the buyer;
- buyer and decision-maker authority;
- confidence in the problem and strength of counter-evidence;
- AMX proof, delivery ability, capacity and dependencies;
- scope clarity, cost, margin, risk and time-to-cash;
- reachability, authorized contact route, payment feasibility and next decision;
- probability of a real useful outcome and the cost of being wrong (Expected Regret).

Use the existing intention weighting model rather than inventing a second score. Missing numeric inputs remain UNKNOWN; don't fabricate precision. Prefer the smallest **meaningful paid result** that solves a real part of the problem, not a paid list of defects when the customer could use an actual finished output. Use a diagnostic only when the diagnostic itself is valuable and precisely scoped.

Every offer must answer in buyer language:
- Why this buyer and why now, using one relevant signal.
- What outcome or completed deliverable they receive.
- What is included and explicitly excluded.
- What proof supports the claim.
- Time/dependencies, customer inputs, acceptance test and applicable IP/usage terms.
- Price, comparable benchmark/source date, currency/tax basis, and payment route.
- One clear, low-friction next step.

Do not sell internal architecture terms when plain buyer outcomes communicate value. Do not lead with an exhaustive service catalogue or a grand explanation of AMX. Do not give away the buyer's bespoke diagnosis, implementation recipe, tailored creative, prompt or solution blueprint as an unpaid teaser; demonstrate quality with an unrelated/synthetic example while giving full clarity on what the paid scope contains.

## 5. Buyer psychology must change the actual offer

The following are mandatory decision behaviours, not prose advice:

| Existing learning / failure evidence | Behaviour the offer process must produce |
|---|---|
| A manual process is not automatically a problem. Cork City Gaol said it was happy with phone/email booking. | Validate whether the process causes material friction, risk, delay or cost before proposing automation. A legitimate “no need” reduces weight for that exact pain hypothesis, not for the entire sector. |
| A cheap offer can still have no value. Cuina Teva declined a low-cost extra-defect list after the first email already disclosed specific issues. | Sell a useful bounded outcome, not “pay us to tell you more things are wrong.” Do not infer that cheaper is automatically easier to buy. |
| A long abstract message creates work for the buyer. Colonia Estudio declined a roughly 204-word, multi-element workflow pitch using internal vocabulary. | Start with one context-specific observation and outcome; test relevance; use natural buyer language, a focused offer and one CTA. Use the existing concise-outreach guidance as a directional control, not a universal word-count law. |
| A competitor being selected does not reveal why. Sakubloom chose another designer and retained the portfolio. | Record competitive loss / cause unknown until evidence supports a reason. Do not globally lower price or rewrite all offers based on invented causality. |
| Contact failure happens before persuasion. Bounces, wrong routes, duplicates and non-delivery exist in the current record. | Give route/delivery failure its own class. It must not be trained as negative offer-copy or buyer-fit evidence. |
| Public presentation may contain signals without proving a defect. | State public-source conclusions as a testable hypothesis; do not shame the prospect, assert hidden motives, or give away the tailored solution. |
| A visible role/title does not prove budget authority. | Resolve the actual evaluator / authorizer. A gatekeeper is a valid routing path, not necessarily the economic buyer. |
| Silence is not consent, rejection or intent. | Follow the existing approved cadence where permitted, stop at opt-out/bounce/suppression, preserve the next due action, and never label silence as a response. |

Public-facing psychology should be visible through relevance and respect, not described to the prospect as a manipulation technique. Build trust by being specific, accurate, useful and easy to respond to.

## 6. Concrete overlooked category: CARBON Studios / vertical short-form drama

Vertical short-form drama is a real commercial category, not a generic “AI video” footnote. The opportunity must be handled as a buyer-and-stage map, not a single assumption that AMX can currently deliver and licence a finished 50-episode slate to every platform.

### Buyer segments and distinct offers

| Buyer / route | Candidate commercial product | Qualification / readiness condition |
|---|---|---|
| Microdrama / streaming platform acquiring finished seasons | Finished, rights-cleared, episode-complete vertical series; licensing, territory/platform rights or revenue-share negotiation | Only approach as a finished-series acquisition when all required episodes, delivery specs, rights and current buyer terms are evidenced. |
| Platform accepting development-stage projects | Controlled sample, logline/bible, pilot, episode outline or co-development proposal | Verify that the buyer accepts development-stage submissions and what they will evaluate. State exactly what is complete vs in development. |
| Production house / funded producer | Fee-for-service vertical production, scripting, episode adaptation, AI-assisted production workflow, post-production or white-label capacity | Confirm there is a funded project or a genuine production brief; qualify budget, schedule, deliverables, rights and acceptance. A vendor is not presumed to be a financier or distributor. |
| Brand / agency / campaign owner | Branded episodic story, product integration, social cutdowns, campaign package, short-form narrative series | Sell a defined brand outcome and a scoped package; qualify brand-fit, approval process, distribution plan, performance measurement and rights. |
| IP owner / publisher / author / creator | Adaptation, script development, vertical-drama conversion, localization, character/series bible and licensing arrangement | Confirm chain of title and adaptation permissions, existing audience/rights, intended buyer and monetization route. |
| Distributor / catalogue partner | Catalogue representation, rights licensing, territory sales, platform introductions or distribution partnership | Define exclusivity, territories, term, deliverables, recoupment, revenue share and reporting. Do not transfer or imply rights without authority. |
| Studio needing repeatable output | Season development, episode-batch delivery, editing/localization/cutdown retainers or multi-title slate | Bound quality, volume, turnaround and unit economics; use a paid pilot with explicit conversion terms before promising scale. |

Potential monetization routes include upfront production/development fees; commissioned series; brand sponsorship and in-story integrations; licensing and territory/platform rights; co-production with transparent recoupment/revenue share; adaptation/localization fees; post-production/cutdown packages; and, only where AMX controls a viable distribution route, direct paid episodes, subscriptions or advertising. ## 7. Volume, cadence and conversion learning

The commercial chain must actively approach enough **distinct, properly selected prospects** to produce meaningful feedback. The statistical premise is straightforward: with no suitable offers reaching buyers, no positive conversion can be observed. Volume without fit, delivery or learning produces noise; fit without offers produces no sales. Both selection and repeated execution matter.

Required controls:
1. Every commercial execution cycle reports **new opportunities qualified, new offers made ready, offers actually sent, due follow-ups completed, replies classified, acceptances, paid/settled outcomes, and any item held with reason/owner/next action**. Do not substitute scans, candidate counts, drafts or reports for offer output.
2. An execution cycle ending with zero buyer-facing offers despite available sale-ready offers and eligible buyer routes is an **exception requiring a reason code and next action**, not a silent success. Reasons may include no qualified buyer fit, contact suppression, capacity, rights, unverified route, missing approved price, payment restriction or a genuine delivery hold.
3. Maintain a predeclared cohort/test unit when comparing alternative offers. Change one material dimension at a time where practical (buyer segment, problem hypothesis, outcome, proof, CTA, package or price). Label small samples as directional, not statistically conclusive.
4. Measure stage-specific rates with explicit denominators: delivered/sent, replies/delivered, qualified replies/replies, offer acceptance/offers issued, paid/accepted offers, settlement/paid, delivery acceptance/deliveries, gross contribution per unit and time-to-cash. Segment by buyer type, offer, route, market and version. Do not combine unlike offers into a meaningless single conversion rate.
5. Learn from **attributable** outcomes. Contact validity updates reachability; a specific rejection updates that offer/problem/buyer hypothesis; price objections update price only when the evidence isolates price; a purchase updates the matching offer/segment; settlement is verified by Banker. Do not double count credit or rewrite unrelated history.
6. Keep the existing follow-up rule (five business days while unresolved and contact is permitted) where active and applicable. Do not reactivate disabled schedulers or invent a new cadence. Reconcile replies, suppression and existing outreach first.
7. Preserve the 65% money-in KPI and 60/20/20 sourcing rule in their separate meanings. Do not turn a KPI weight into a per-worker sales mandate, nor let sourcing percentages block inbound offers.
8. Do not set arbitrary numeric quotas in this addendum. ROOT/owner-approved capacity and deliverability should set the batch size; absence of a configured target must be surfaced and routed, not silently converted to zero activity.

## 8. Learning classes and weight updates

Each outcome must be assigned to its true cause before weighting:
- **NO SIGNAL / NO RESPONSE:** weak evidence; keep silence separate from explicit rejection.
- **BOUNCE / WRONG ROUTE / DUPLICATE:** route and execution correction; do not penalize offer quality.
- **GATEKEEPER / REFERRED:** update authority routing; do not count as buyer rejection.
- **EXPLICIT NO — NO MATERIAL NEED:** negative weight to the exact problem hypothesis/buyer/use case; retain counterexamples and do not generalize to an entire industry without evidence.
- **EXPLICIT NO — PRICE / BUDGET:** update only if buyer evidence actually identifies price or budget as the cause; test scope/value/payment timing and comparable alternatives before cutting price.
- **EXPLICIT NO — TIMING / EXISTING SOLUTION / COMPETITOR:** classify separately; preserve a permissible future route if one exists, but respect STOP and offer-specific suppression.
- **INTEREST / CLARIFICATION / SCOPE REQUEST:** advance the actual requested next step; interest is not acceptance.
- **ACCEPTED / CONTRACTED / INVOICED / PAID / SETTLED / DELIVERED / ACCEPTED DELIVERY:** separate state changes with evidence.
- **DELIVERY FAILURE / QUALITY DEFECT:** feed delivery quality and estimation, not merely acquisition copy.
- **REFERRAL / REPEAT ORDER / EXPANSION:** record origin and incremental contribution; do not claim causal lift absent evidence.

The owner feedback and outcome must change the next selection, offer or experiment. Repeating the same buyer assumption after a clear correction is an intention-engine enforcement failure. Preserve raw response/evidence, classification, confidence, the weight/action changed, model/core version and readback.

## 9. Required implementation and acceptance tests

**Owner of integration:** ROOT/T-GOV disposition; MASTER/LIBRARIAN controls provenance and integration checkpoints; no fourth builder. Shared intent-core/code changes belong to T-COD through the existing #18 route. Commercial duties remain with iSCOPE → PRI; delivery feasibility remains with the existing production/service owner; Banker owns costs and money truth.

Before claiming implementation, the owning runtime must demonstrate at least these behavioral tests:
1. A request that literally asks for one action but clearly seeks a broader commercial outcome chooses the outcome-serving next action without exceeding authority.
2. The capability inventory emits buyer/product pairings and viable offers; sale-ready capabilities do not stay hidden merely because no prospect explicitly asked for them.
3. Observation of a manual/ugly/long process alone does not become asserted buyer pain.
4. The same capability produces different scoped offers for materially different buyers/stages where justified.
5. The offer is outcome-first, proof-backed, customer-readable, bounded, economically checked and has one clear next step.
6. A bounce, wrong route, no response, referral, explicit rejection, price objection and competitor selection update different evidence dimensions.
7. Rejection of one offer does not poison all future offers or the entire sector; STOP and contact boundaries remain enforced.
8. Vertical-drama buyer-stage mismatch is caught: no finished-season acquisition claim without an evidenced finished, rights-cleared, spec-compliant season; suitable development-stage, paid-pilot, brand and production-service alternatives are considered.
9. Existing microdrama threads, suppression and due-follow-up boundaries are reconciled before any new contact.
10. Price source/date/scope/currency/tax/factor, immutable Offer ID/version, quote readback, send receipt and explicit acceptance are linked.
11. An empty offer cycle with eligible offer/buyers is flagged; an empty cycle with a true hold has owner, reason and next action.
12. Outcome changes feed the **same shared intention core** and its authorized consumers; no duplicate scoring model or prompt-only implementation.
13. Current version passes relevant regression tests, is deployed where intended, and the exact runtime/consumer is read back.
14. Conversion reports distinguish attributed progress, buyer acceptance, invoicing, payment, settlement and delivery acceptance.

**Implementation passes only with evidence:** tests and outputs, revision/version, deployed runtime identity where applicable, consumer-by-consumer readback, a persisted offer-state transition, and observed downstream outcome/weight behavior. A new file, prompt amendment, ticket comment or successful tool call alone is not acceptance.
