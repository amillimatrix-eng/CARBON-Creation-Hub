# BLUE STATE / OPERATING RULE PROPOSAL — RISK-PROPORTIONAL COMMERCIAL TRUST GATES

**Intake ID:** ROOT-20261009-MONEY-IN-RISK-PROPORTIONAL-TRUST  
**Recommended classification:** BLUE STATE / OPERATING RULE  
**Evidence trigger:** Ecognix thread, 2026-09-27  
**Authority effect:** PROPOSAL ONLY — Governance decides promotion.

## Anomaly

AMX inserted an identity/authorization escalation into a warm buyer-request thread after the buyer had requested a synthetic sample and exact thread continuity was strongly corroborated.

The requested material contained no buyer data, credentials, staging access, private endpoints or client findings. Higher-risk commercial steps had not yet occurred.

This created avoidable friction and produced a contradictory internal state: the system described the artifact as held after it had already been transmitted.

## Commercial consequence

Unnecessary verification at a low-risk reversible stage can delay or derail warm demand before scope/payment is reached.

The Ecognix rejection was explicitly attributed by the buyer to already having a QA team. Therefore this proposal does **not** claim the trust escalation caused the rejection.

## Proposed rule

**TRUST CONTROLS SCALE WITH CONSEQUENCE.**

For an explicitly requested low-risk artifact that is:
- synthetic, public, or otherwise non-sensitive;
- reversible;
- delivered in a strongly corroborated existing buyer thread;
- not granting credentials, private access, regulated data, money movement, legal authority or binding commitment;

the system may deliver the artifact under ordinary commercial thread continuity without requiring a separate identity/contracting-entity verification gate.

Identity / authority verification becomes mandatory before the first materially consequential step requiring it, including:
- staging/system access;
- credentials/secrets;
- private client data;
- regulated or sensitive information;
- binding legal commitment;
- contracting entity creation/acceptance;
- invoicing/payment where counterparty identity matters;
- rights transfer;
- material irreversible action.

**THREAD CONTINUITY != LEGAL AUTHORITY.**  
But **LACK OF FULL LEGAL-AUTHORITY PROOF != AUTOMATIC HOLD FOR LOW-RISK REQUESTED ARTIFACT DELIVERY.**

## Conflict check

This does not weaken security governance, DOM8N, privacy rules, KYC, legal gates, or provider safety restrictions.

It complements existing dependency/route validation by defining proportional timing.

## Acceptance test

PASS when:
1. low-risk requested samples flow without unnecessary identity escalation;
2. sensitive/high-consequence steps still trigger exact verification;
3. the same opportunity does not oscillate between RELEASED and HELD because the gate was applied after the fact;
4. no private/sensitive information is released under the low-risk exception;
5. buyer-thread friction is reduced without increasing security incidents.

## Rollback / narrowing condition

If evidence shows the low-risk rule materially increases fraud, unauthorized disclosure, contractual confusion or security incidents, Governance must narrow the allowed artifact classes or raise verification earlier for the affected class.
