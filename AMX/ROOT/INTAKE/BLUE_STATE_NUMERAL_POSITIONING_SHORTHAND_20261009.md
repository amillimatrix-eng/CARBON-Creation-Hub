## Core rule

A **bare numeral sent on its own**, when the active conversational task is explanation, reframing, interpretation, or presentation, is an instruction to **reposition the immediately preceding subject to that age-like explanation level**.

Examples:

- `10` → explain for an intelligent 10-year-old;
- `15` → explain for a teenager with mechanisms but limited jargon;
- `23` → explain for a young adult / early-career professional;
- `33` → explain for an experienced adult professional with systems, trade-offs and strategic implications.

The numeral operates as a **positioning control**, not as a claim about the Owner's literal age.

## What changes

The shorthand may change:

- vocabulary;
- abstraction level;
- amount of assumed domain knowledge;
- analogy density;
- commercial/professional framing;
- systems depth;
- trade-off discussion;
- sentence complexity;
- contextual examples.

## What must NOT change

The shorthand must not alter:

- underlying facts;
- evidence standards;
- uncertainty;
- provenance;
- authority boundaries;
- governance state;
- safety constraints;
- commercial truth;
- technical truth;
- acceptance/payment state;
- conclusions unsupported by evidence.

**POSITIONING MAY CHANGE. TRUTH MAY NOT.**

## Default referent

Unless another referent is explicit, the numeral applies to the **immediately preceding subject or answer**.

A new explanation should preserve the substantive conclusion while changing only the positioning layer required by the numeral.

## Interpretation precedence

The shorthand must not steal meaning from a number that is clearly being used literally.

Use the numeral-positioning shorthand when:

1. the message is only a numeral or an unmistakably bare numeric positioning token; and
2. the active conversational context is explanation/reframing/presentation; and
3. no stronger local numeric interpretation is evidenced.

Treat the numeral literally instead when the surrounding context clearly establishes it as:

- a quantity;
- calculation input or result;
- item/list number;
- version/build number;
- identifier;
- age being discussed as factual data;
- score;
- price;
- percentage;
- date/time;
- duration;
- model/worker count;
- command parameter;
- other domain-specific numeric value.

If both meanings remain genuinely plausible, preserve continuity and use the strongest contextual evidence rather than mechanically forcing the shorthand.

## Continuity rule

Once this shorthand has been established, the Owner does **not** need to restate "explain this like a 10-year-old / 15-year-old / 23-year-old / 33-year-old" each time.

A bare numeral is sufficient where the positioning interpretation is contextually valid.

Repeated numeral requests are stateful reformulations of the same subject unless the conversation has materially moved to a new subject.

## Anti-drift rule

Do not interpret the numeral as permission to:

- dumb down facts;
- patronize;
- stereotype people of that literal age;
- omit material caveats solely to make the answer simpler;
- invent certainty;
- replace evidence with analogy;
- alter the Matrix state;
- reinterpret the Owner's identity.

The numeral controls **communication position**, not evidence or ontology.

## Example sequence

Subject: AMX revenue inflow mechanics.

- Owner sends `10` → simple concrete explanation.
- Owner sends `15` → same facts, more mechanism.
- Owner sends `23` → same facts, early-professional framing.
- Owner sends `33` → same facts, experienced professional / systems framing.

The sequence does not require the Owner to re-specify the method.
