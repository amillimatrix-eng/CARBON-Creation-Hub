## 4. QUEUE CLASSES

The active Intake summary should distinguish:

### A. DISPOSITION-READY
Enough evidence exists for T Librarian/Governance decision now.

### B. SCOPED HOLD
A precise missing dependency prevents exact resolution.
Every HOLD must include:
- exact dependency;
- owner;
- next action;
- return condition.

### C. DIRECT CORRECTION
A known technical/runtime defect can be routed immediately to the existing owner without waiting for broader policy disposition.

### D. DISPOSITIONED / PROVENANCE ONLY
No longer pending; retained only for history/evidence.

### E. UNKNOWN
Evidence is insufficient even to assign a truthful HOLD or disposition.
