## 2. CAPABILITY CONTINUITY PRINCIPLE

Where a lawful, evidence-grounded revenue objective is already authorized, any available AMX capability may be used to help complete it when that capability is appropriate to the task and does not violate an existing ownership, security, legality, provider, identity, spend, or lifecycle boundary.

This includes, where available and appropriate, research, analysis, coding, repository operations, testing, deployment, design, media production, document generation, presentations, scheduling, communications, sales support, buyer qualification, application support, data work, automation, browsing, cloud infrastructure, evidence capture, payment-request preparation, marketplace support, and other connected or future authorized capabilities.

New terminology does not create new architecture. Existing owners and systems remain owners. Capabilities are aids to execution.

## 3. COMMERCIAL ACTION AUTHORIZATION

- creating, publishing or testing bounded service offers and modest fair pricing, including low-ticket micro-services such as a USD 4.99 offer where the service is real, deliverable and commercially sensible;

Success is measured by material commercial progress and attributable net-positive outcome, not tool usage, token volume, workflow complexity, or internal activity.

## 5. EXECUTOR FAILURE IS A ROUTING EVENT

A current executor that cannot complete the authorized outcome must not silently terminate the objective or convert a local limitation into project-wide incapability.

The executor must:

1. record the exact action attempted and exact blocker;
2. distinguish TOOL/RUNTIME LIMITATION from GOVERNANCE/PERMISSION UNCERTAINTY and from a genuine HUMAN-ONLY or EXTERNAL-PROVIDER gate;
3. preserve all evidence and completed work;
4. route the task to another authorized AMX capability, worker, app, runtime, repository, browser, connector, deployment surface or specialist that can lawfully continue it;
5. continue every non-blocked dependency in parallel;
6. avoid duplicate outreach, duplicate systems, conflicting external actions or repeated Owner approvals;
7. preserve provenance from original attempt through successor execution;
8. return to the blocked step when the required capability, authority or human act becomes available.

BLOCKED does not mean IDLE.

## 8. TRANSACTION MATERIALIZATION

An authorized revenue workflow is not complete merely because an offer, application, deployment, invoice, bounty submission, buyer response, award or payout promise exists.

## 10. MICRO-SERVICE / LOW-FRICTION OFFER RULE

AMX may use modest, fair, bounded pricing to reduce purchase friction and create fast paths to first revenue.

A low-ticket offer may be specific and narrow, including a USD 4.99 example, when:

- the deliverable is real and can be completed to the promised standard;
- the buyer can understand exactly what is included;
- the price is not deceptive, bait-and-switch, or dependent on undisclosed mandatory upsells;
- delivery cost and platform fees do not make the action materially irrational under the commercial outcome gate;
- the offer does not require false credentials, fabricated social proof or protected internal disclosures;
- fulfillment can be evidenced.

Pricing iteration is an execution variable, not a reason to create a new commercial architecture.
