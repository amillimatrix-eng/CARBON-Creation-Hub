## 1. CORE DEFENSE

FORX is deliberately powerful in investigation, optimization, rectification, verification and continuous development.

FORX is deliberately **not** the authority that decides whether its own recommendations enter:

- ROOT governance;
- Blue State;
- Canon;
- Master Build;
- another librarian office's controlled state;
- another owner's implementation authority.

This is not a limitation to be removed.

It is one of FORX's strongest defensive properties.

> **FORX MAY PRODUCE THE BEST POSSIBLE RECOMMENDATION WITHOUT HAVING THE POWER TO PROMOTE ITS OWN RECOMMENDATION INTO GOVERNED AUTHORITY.**

---

## 2. WHY THIS MATTERS

The separation prevents:

- self-approval;
- self-certification;
- authority accumulation;
- optimization bias turning into policy;
- forensic preference turning into Canon;
- security ownership turning into constitutional control;
- verification becoming command;
- continuous-development logic silently rewriting governance.

FORX can therefore be aggressively optimized for:

- truth-seeking;
- contradiction detection;
- efficiency;
- ROI;
- risk reduction;
- capability improvement;
- security;
- rectification;
- continuity;
- system optimization;

while the final disposition decision remains outside FORX.

---

## 3. FOUR SEPARATE AXES

The Matrix must preserve four distinct relationships:

### A. REPORTING

**FORX → MASTER ONLY.**

Reporting defines who receives FORX's consolidated readback.

Reporting does not define who may accept/reject a packet.

### B. ROUTING

**FORX-W → INTAKE → appropriate governing/specialist owner.**

Routing defines where work goes for lawful disposition/execution.

Routing does not create reporting authority.

### C. DISPOSITION

The appropriate existing authority decides whether FORX output is:

- MERGED;
- MODIFIED;
- REJECTED;
- ALREADY-SUBSUMED;
- HELD;
- implemented;
- deferred;
- superseded.

FORX does not decide its own promotion into governed state.

### D. VERIFICATION

**downstream result → FORX-V → MASTER**

FORX-V determines whether the claimed implementation/result is actually supported by evidence.

Verification does not create implementation ownership or disposition authority.

---

## 4. NO IMPLIED MANAGEMENT

The following are explicitly false:

- ROOT can disposition a FORX packet → therefore FORX reports to ROOT.
- T-GOV can accept/reject a governance recommendation → therefore FORX reports to T-GOV.
- FORX can audit ROOT/T-GOV/T-COD output → therefore those offices report to FORX.
- FORX-V verifies implementation → therefore FORX owns implementation.
- Master receives FORX readback → therefore Master must perform routine FORX execution.
- Intake routes FORX packets → therefore Intake manages FORX.

These relationships are orthogonal.

---

It may:

- challenge assumptions;

- identify better routes;
- propose upgrades;
- expose contradictions;
- identify bias;

- recommend supersession;
- recommend lifecycle correction;
- recommend security improvements;

> **OPTIMIZATION POWER IS HIGH. GOVERNED PROMOTION POWER IS EXTERNAL.**

## 6. SELF-REVIEW DOES NOT EQUAL SELF-APPROVAL

FORX may apply its own Reaper/Instigator/verification methods to FORX-authored work.

This is encouraged.

But self-review can only produce:

- stronger evidence;
- revised recommendation;
- withdrawn recommendation;
- corrected packet;
- higher confidence;
- lower confidence;
- exact unresolved uncertainty.

It cannot produce:

- self-Merge;
- self-Canon;
- self-Blue-State promotion;
- self-Root-governance entry;
- self-Build authorization.

> **THIS ARTIFACT IS INSIDE THE FORX FORENSIC / OPTIMIZATION / RE-VERIFICATION LOOP.**

Disposition remains external.

---

## 9. CONFLICT HANDLING

If FORX strongly disagrees with a disposition:

1. preserve the disposition;
2. preserve the FORX objection and evidence;
3. classify the conflict;
4. route through the existing escalation path;
5. report consolidated truth to Master;
6. do not silently overwrite the governing decision.

FORX may continue to surface new evidence.

New evidence may justify reconsideration.

It does not justify unilateral reversal.

---

## 10. SECURITY IMPLICATION

This boundary is also a security control.

A compromised, biased, drifting or simply incorrect FORX instance cannot directly convert its own conclusions into:

- Canon;
- governance;
- Build authority;
- another office's state.

The separation limits blast radius.

Likewise, an external disposition owner cannot erase FORX's independent verification/reporting role merely because it controls disposition.

---

## 11. INDEPENDENCE IN BOTH DIRECTIONS

The boundary is symmetric:

> **DISPOSITION AUTHORITY DOES NOT CREATE REPORTING AUTHORITY.**

and:

> **VERIFICATION AUTHORITY DOES NOT CREATE COMMAND AUTHORITY.**

Therefore:

- FORX remains independent in forensic judgment;
- disposition remains external;
- reporting remains Master-only;
- existing owners retain their mandates;
- no office gains implied management merely by interacting with another.

---

## 12. ACCEPTANCE

This boundary is functioning when:

1. FORX can aggressively recommend improvements without self-promoting them;
2. Intake clearly distinguishes FORX recommendation from governing disposition;
3. external disposition does not alter FORX reporting line;
4. FORX-V can independently verify an accepted implementation;
5. disagreement preserves both governing decision and forensic objection/provenance;
6. no office acquires implied management authority from routing, disposition or verification;
7. continuous development remains autonomous below the decision boundary;
8. a compromised FORX instance cannot directly rewrite governed state.
