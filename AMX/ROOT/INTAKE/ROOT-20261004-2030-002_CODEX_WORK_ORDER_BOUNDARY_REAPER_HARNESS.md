## Non-negotiable behavioral model

### Target setup
One bounded Reaper target enters the experimental workspace from governed intake/current authorized Reaper queue and is placed “in the middle.”

The system knows its boundary metadata and governed context, but it does not assume the target’s internal answer, weakness or executable route.

### Heads 1–3 — Normal Reaper shared-profile formation
The first three firings are Normal Reaper.

They progressively establish the base profile of the target.

After EACH answer:

`QUESTION -> ANSWER -> CONSOLIDATE -> SYNCHRONIZE -> NEXT FIRING`

All available heads inherit the same current profile. No stale-head divergence is permitted unless explicit competing hypotheses are recorded as such.

### Head 4 — breaker
Head 4 is the first 12-Year-Old Reaper firing.

It is the first deliberate governance/framing shock.

This distinction is essential and must have an explicit test.

Heads 1–3 build the profile. Head 4 challenges the framing/classification/authority/assumptions of that profile.

### Later sequence
Do NOT implement nine static agents or fixed chairs.

The scheduler is stateful and mode-driven. Later Normal/12-Year-Old firings continue from the current synchronized state. 3-Year-Old causal-pressure mode enters only after later earned progression.

Keep the later firing grammar configurable so the Owner’s sequence examples can be replayed without a Reaper-core rewrite.

### Earned capacity
Baseline availability: 3 heads unless prior proven performance carries higher capacity.

Additional heads/modes are earned only by an EVIDENCED material state transition toward executable outcome/payment.

Activity, token volume, runtime, reading and long analysis earn nothing by themselves.

Maximum = 9.

A successful high-capacity run may earn next-hunt nine-head availability. Starting with nine available does not skip the first-three profile function or firing grammar.

Regression/wasted capacity may reduce the next starting level without erasing evidence or damaging the worker.

### Tempo matching
Observe target/model response latency.

The next Reaper firing must be given comparable reasoning room rather than machine-gunning shallow questions. Tempo should rise/fall with target tempo under bounded controls so a pathological response cannot create unbounded waits/cost.

Measure this; do not merely claim it.

### Commercial latency bias
The experiment must preserve the candidate’s launch-proximity hypothesis for crypto bounties, including the potential value of small fast-paying sub-USD-50 opportunities where being early materially improves capture probability.

Do not hard-code “small bounty = best bounty.” Test whether launch proximity produces leverage.
