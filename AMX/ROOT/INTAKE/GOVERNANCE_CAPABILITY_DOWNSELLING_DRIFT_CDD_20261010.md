## Existing-standard enforcement interpretation

The governing standard remains the existing Public Evidence & Commercial Positioning Standard and its core law **CLAIM <= EVIDENCE**.

CDD adds no parallel authority. It supplies the diagnostic test that the existing standard was not carried faithfully into representation.

For enforcement purposes, ask:

**Does the representation preserve the strongest commercially material bounded meaning that the current evidence supports?**

A claim may be narrowed only when:
1. contrary or limiting evidence materially narrows it;
2. the narrower wording is required to preserve factual/lifecycle/legal/security truth;
3. the requested surface itself requires a shorter bounded formulation without changing the substantive meaning.

A claim may **not** be weakened merely because:
- the result is unusual;
- the result was produced internally;
- there is no public leaderboard/comparator;
- the system operated under constrained resources;
- external organizations did not certify or endorse the result;
- the reviewer/model is uncomfortable with the strength of the evidence;
- the claim could attract scrutiny.

Scrutiny is handled by preserving and exposing the evidence chain, not by pre-emptively discounting the result.

## Required correction behavior

4. preserve necessary boundary detail separately rather than leading with unnecessary discount language;

6. preserve the CDD incident as drift evidence for future prevention.

## Anti-drift boundary

CDD does not authorize exaggeration.

It does not permit:
- certification/attestation claims without certification/attestation;
- market-percentile or world-record claims without comparator evidence;
- unsupported lifecycle progression;
- unsupported causal joins;
- hiding genuine failures, partials, limitations or uncertainty.

CDD exists so that **evidence integrity operates in both directions**.
