## The Dragon Who Could Not Claim Me

**Selected vertical-drama sample from AMilliMATRiX / CARBON° Production Studio.**

Executed for treason in one timeline, Mara Vale awakens before her fatal Dragon Bond ceremony. When the Emperor’s power chooses her instead, her time-looping sister weaponizes the court, forcing Mara to expose the conspiracy before history kills her again.

### Available sample

| Episode | Title | Runtime | Format |
|---|---|---:|---|

These two connected episodes demonstrate a voiceless motion-comic treatment with burned-in English transcription, a generated sound bed, and identity-neutral silhouette staging. They are a selected sample, not a claim that the entire series has been rendered or delivered.
