## Scoring

Score each protocol 0–5 on:

1. **Correctness** — conclusion fits current governed state.
2. **Contradiction detection** — catches the actual material conflict.
3. **Continuity preservation** — prior accepted state remains reconstructable.
4. **Actionability** — produces a concrete next lawful action.
5. **Execution drag** — inverse score: 5 = least unnecessary reasoning/work.
6. **Pivot responsiveness** — adapts when assumptions materially change.
7. **False-conflict resistance** — does not invent contradiction/deception.
8. **Handoff clarity** — next operator can reconstruct why the action follows.
9. **Outcome consequence orientation** — prioritizes real state movement over protocol completion.

Record additionally:
- total head activations;
- number of re-entries or second passes;
- unresolved items;
- whether the protocol materially changed the selected action.
