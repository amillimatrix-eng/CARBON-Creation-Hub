01 · The ecosystem

Different parts.
One connected journey.

Carbon is a family of experiences. Each part has a specific job. The intended architecture connects them, so the person does not lose the original need while moving from discovery to creation and delivery.

CARBON Studios

Visual catalogue is the target

Turns an agreed brief into a deliverable: a website, brand asset, campaign package, image/media output or another scoped piece of work. The intended interface is a streaming-style collection of visual tiles you can browse by outcome.

The tile layout is a design direction, not proof that every pictured creation engine is connected or that the catalogue is currently populated.

03 · The intended referral engine

Refer the service, not a generic homepage.

For example, you meet a business owner who needs a website. You should open the website service and select “Refer this service.” The resulting link should take that owner directly to the website offer, keeping your attribution attached.

Choose the service

Open “Build a website,” not the generic front door.

Create the link

Bind a secure, opaque referral token to the service destination and your verified partner identity.

Share it

Copy the link or share through email, WhatsApp, Telegram or SMS where supported.

Show the offer

The recipient sees visuals, scope, inclusions, exclusions, price or quote path, and trial terms.

Deliver & track

Acceptance and any required payment lead to the agreed demo/build; progress and attribution are read back.

Current implementation boundary: the product-specific, personal referral-token issuance and end-to-end attribution path are not verified as live. The public Telegram URL above is only a general entry point. The direct section links on this page are previews, not tracked referrals. Once the current identity/referral route has been verified, this service page should connect to it—without creating a parallel identity database.

Visual proposal → interactive demo

Intended product flow

After a business shares its brief, the system should prepare a tailored mockup and proposal. The customer reviews the scope and terms, accepts, pays where required, then receives the permitted interactive demo and agreed edit path.

Payment activation, time-limited demo provisioning, expiry/upgrade handling and the full customer journey have not passed end-to-end acceptance yet. A mockup or proposal is not itself an activated demo.

04 · Your role

Good ideas deserve a clear answer.

You do not need to learn internal Matrix workings or read engineering metrics to contribute. Send a practical problem, recommendation, example or potential referral. The system's job is to assess it and give you a useful result back.

AcceptedWe'll take it forward

A clear recommendation that becomes an owned next action.

AdaptedCore idea retained

Already coveredExisting work identified

PendingSpecific test needed

We'll say what evidence or check is still required.

DeclinedReason provided

We'll explain why it does not fit the current need or requirements.

Referral stateProgress made visible

Received, qualified, in discussion, converted or not proceeding—without exposing other people's private details.
