## 1. Decision to preserve

The current product-convergence blueprint defines the product relationship as:

- **CARBON° / Carbon° Business naming:** the convergence blueprint treats CARBON° as the product/experience layer, while the 14 October owner-approved visual-continuity invariant calls the umbrella name **Carbon° Business**. Those source statements need reconciliation before public copy treats the exact umbrella wording as settled. The repair uses the existing CARBON° wordmark and avoids turning this discrepancy into a visitor-facing ambiguity.
- **Search / INTELLAGENT** — resolves a stated need into measured intent.
- **Marketplace** — matches intent to listings or offers.
- **Studios** — creative production and fulfilment.
- **AMilliMATRiX** — continuity, governance, evidence, routing and trust.

The current live landing page presents six capability degrees: Business°, Design°, Intelligence°, Systems°, Utility° and Studios°. This describes the live UI, not a resolution of the conflicting umbrella-name statements above. In the repair, “Business Services” means the existing customer-facing service-entry route; its name should not be used to silently decide the master-brand hierarchy.

**CREATE is a workflow verb/action.** Current landing-source inspection found no independent “Carbon Create” product label in the landing HTML or a working /create route. Do not present “Carbon Create” as a distinct product unless a canonical owner approves the product identity, a real destination exists, and its capability passes acceptance.

“Business Services” is the clearest existing name for the business-facing service-entry layer. That phrase describes where a customer finds and scopes service work; it does not establish a separate umbrella brand or an implemented business application.

## 2. First-view copy proposal

The Owner's 2026-10-21 direction authorizes repairs consequential to CARBON°'s perceived public position. The bounded copy below is being implemented against verified current capabilities; it must not be expanded into unsupported claims or an invented standalone product.

**Headline**

> Turn a business need into a usable outcome.

**Supporting copy**

> CARBON° scopes and delivers websites, commercial presentation, digital workflows, software and creative production around the result you need. Start with a defined service or describe the outcome you are trying to achieve; scope and terms are confirmed before custom work begins.

**Primary action:** “Explore services” → the existing live partner/service guide, /carbon/partner.  
**Secondary action:** “See production work” → the existing Studios/work section or a verified work-detail route.  
**Optional explanatory action:** “How CARBON° fits together” → a short explanation of CARBON° as the umbrella, capability areas as disciplines, and Search → Marketplace → Studios as the intended intent-to-fulfilment flow.

Avoid leading the first view with “UNIVERSAL CREATION + PRODUCTION HOUSE” alone. It sounds broad but does not quickly answer what a customer can commission now, where to start, or what remains a preview. The existing premium visual identity should be preserved; the change is information hierarchy and route truth, not a visual rebuild.
