# AMX continuity handoff

Trigger: current interactive execution surface becomes unavailable or exhausted.

Continuity rule: implementation continues after the conversation ends. A durable receipt, not conversational completion, establishes a material transition.

Recovery:
1. Read overdrive/state.json and overdrive/opportunities.json.
2. Read newest overdrive/receipts and open repository issues.
3. Inspect latest workflow runs.
4. Resume from the furthest evidenced state; do not redo completed work.
5. Preserve separate commercial states through PAID.
6. Read organization history before outreach and do not duplicate existing approaches.
7. Persist material transitions as receipts.
8. Use another authorized execution surface when one transport is unavailable.

Current durable surfaces: this repository, OVERDRIVE state, opportunity ledger, receipts, workflow history, and open remediation issues.

A handoff is verified only after state readback and a subsequent real receipt.
